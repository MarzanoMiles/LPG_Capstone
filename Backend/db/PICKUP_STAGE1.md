Run `node db/migrate_pickup_stage1.js` from Backend before deploying the updated application. The migration is repeatable and adds PickupStatus, PickupScheduledAt, and PickupSettlementPending to Order. Existing Ready pickups map to Ready for Pickup; existing Completed orders remain completed. No missing historical schedule is fabricated. Such records require manual review before status actions.

Expiration uses CompanySettings BusinessDays, OpeningTime, ClosingTime and PickupExpirationDays. The scheduled date is day zero. The deadline is closing time on the Nth subsequent configured business day, in Philippine time. At or after the deadline, backend list/detail queries and pickup actions evaluate expiration and persist Unclaimed. No client timer, stock adjustment, or refund is involved. Settings changes apply to pending pickups when next evaluated.

GET /orders/pickup returns only paid active pickup rows for the authenticated company, with settlement permission metadata. PUT /orders/:id/pickup-status accepts status Ready for Pickup, Completed, or Cancelled. It checks role, company ownership, payment, deadline and transition under a row lock. Retrying an already-applied status succeeds without another write. Completed pickups leave the active list. Cancelled pickups retain payment/items with settlement pending. Restock & Settle is informational until Stage 2; empty-cylinder returns are deferred to Stage 3.

POS pickup creation is updated only to validate/save pickup schedules against Settings and initialize Preparing. Delivery, customer and non-pickup behavior is unchanged.
